Our story

A family tradition, fried golden

Banu Rita's Savouries began at the family home on Jenkyn Street in Paradise, Newcastle — where the recipes were passed down through generations and every batch is still rolled, spiced and fried the way it always has been.

From hand-rolled mutton samoosas and golden spring rolls to burfee, ladoo and coconut ice for special occasions, everything is made fresh in small batches — because the old-fashioned way just tastes better.

Whether it's a family gathering, a party platter or a sweet treat for the week, we'd love to cook for you.
